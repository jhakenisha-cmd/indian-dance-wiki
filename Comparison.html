Comparison of Forms

Table below illustrates differences between dance forms.

Dance Form Region Focus
Bharatanatyam Tamil Nadu Precision, geometric patterns, and a balance of Nritya (pure dance) and Abhinaya (expression).
Kathak Northern India Intricate footwork (Tatkar), lightning-fast spins (Chakkars), and rhythmic storytelling.
Odissi Odisha Fluidity, the iconic S-shaped Tribhanga posture, and sculpturesque movements.
Kathakali Kerela High drama, elaborate facial expressions, intense martial arts-inspired movement, and complex "green" makeup/costumes.
